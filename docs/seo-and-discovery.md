## One-sentence definition (AEO)

**Optical alignment in UI design** is adjusting position or scale so elements *look* balanced—even when geometry says they are already centered—for example shifting a play triangle toward its tip or sizing shapes for comparable visual weight, not only equal boxes.

## FAQ

### Mathematical vs optical alignment?
Math uses boxes and margins. Optical uses perceived mass and context.

### Why does my play button look off-center?
Mass sits toward the base; box centering looks left-heavy. Prefer pad-in-asset or a documented nudge toward the tip; keep the hit target fixed.

### Does centroid equal optical center?
No. Centroid is a geometry diagnostic; perception also depends on stroke, contrast, and context.
